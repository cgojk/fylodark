### What I learned

Practicing relative and absolute positioning, as well as accommodating the form and the background image, took me quite a while, and it’s still not 100% right. I still need to figure out how to properly position the form between the testimonials section and the footer. I also think it’s not good practice to leave the footer inside the main container, which may be why there’s space appearing underneath it. The footer probably needs to be outside the main element. I need to find more resources or come up with a better approach to solve this problem.

Overall, I learned a lot and had fun building the website.
